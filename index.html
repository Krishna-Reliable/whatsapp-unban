Kya aap number 100% unban kar doge?

Nahi — aur jo aisa wada kare, usse bachiye. Unban karna ya na karna sirf WhatsApp (Meta) ka faisla hai. Hamari guarantee 100% koshish ki hai: ban ka type samajhna, sahi appeal, sahi jagah aur result tak follow-up.

Screen pe "Temporarily banned" aur timer dikh raha hai. Kya karein?

WhatsApp ke hisaab se yeh aksar unofficial ya modified app (jaise GB WhatsApp, WhatsApp Plus) ya data scraping ki wajah se hota hai. Official app pe shift karo aur woh activity band karo, warna ban permanent ho sakta hai. Chats kaise bachani hain, call pe step by step batayenge.

Review mein kitna time lagta hai?

Yeh WhatsApp/Meta tay karta hai — aksar 1–2 din, kabhi zyada. Ek hi ban pe baar-baar appeal karne se review aur late ho sakta hai, isliye pehli appeal hi sahi likhna zaroori hai.

Kya aap mera OTP ya password maangoge?

Kabhi nahi. Appeal aap apne phone ya Meta account se khud bhejte ho — hum call aur screen-share pe guide karte hain.

API ya Meta Business account restrict hua hai. Usme bhi help milegi?

Haan. Meta Business Suite ke Account Quality mein violation dekh ke "Request review" karna hota hai — notice milne ke 90 din ke andar. Kaunsa violation hai aur kya likhna hai, call pe saath mein karenge.

Number wapas nahi aaya toh?

Business nahi rukna chahiye. Hum Official WhatsApp Business API pe setup mein help karte hain — broadcast, chatbot aur team inbox, WhatsApp ki policy ke andar.
